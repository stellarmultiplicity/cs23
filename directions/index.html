Registration

We are pleased to open submission for two different types of contributions! During Block 1 we invite researchers to give short five minute contributions presenting emerging results or open questions in Cool Stellar Multiplicity. During Block 2 we invite researchers to give short 6-10 minute talks on the subject of future missions involving Cool Stellar Multiplicity. Moderators will compile a list of challenges from these talks and discussions for the later Discussion session. We will also have a small poster-pop session at the end of Block 1 to highlight attendees with posters to present in the subsequent coffee break. Abstract submission opening soon.

Important Dates

-**Abstract Submission Deadline:** 7th April -**Notice of acceptance:** TBC -**Finalised programme posted:** 1st May

Venue

The session will be hosted at the CoolStars23 conference venue: TOC Ariake. Room details will be shared later.
